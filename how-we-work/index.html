You don't jump to an AI workforce. You build to it. Each stage makes the next one safe.

Questions

How the engagement actually runs

Do we have to start at Implement?
No. We meet you at your actual stage. If your foundation is solid, we start at Optimize or Agentify. Most teams are a stage earlier than they think — we tell you the truth about that up front.
How is this cheaper than a traditional SI?
We use AI agents throughout delivery — discovery, configuration, testing, documentation — with senior architects directing and reviewing. Fewer hours to the same outcome, passed through as fixed-bid pricing. Typically 30–75% lower cost.
Where does the AI layer fit?
Agentify and AI Workforce are the last two stages, and only durable on a managed foundation. We will not deploy agents onto a broken data model — that just automates the mess.
Who owns the systems when you are done?
You do. Everything is documented and owned. Managed services are a choice, not a lock-in.
