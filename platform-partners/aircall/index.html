Why partner with us

Why Aircall customers choose SaaScend

Voice is where deals are won and lost, and most of it never reaches the CRM. We implement Aircall as a first-class part of the GTM stack: intelligent routing, tight CRM synchronization, and voice AI that captures, coaches, and automates the follow-up work after every call.

  • Voice treated as core GTM data, not a disconnected phone system.
  • Deep CRM synchronization into Salesforce and HubSpot, kept accurate automatically.
  • Voice AI that turns conversations into coaching and pipeline signal.
  • Post-call work automated so reps spend time selling, not logging.
  • One team for the telephony integration and the AI layer on top.
